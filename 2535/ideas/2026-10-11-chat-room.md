# 2026-10-11 — Online Chat Room

## Context
Need real-time consultation with peers for complex decisions.
Multiple participants should be able to join discussions when needed.

## Proposal
Use the shared board system for:
- Real-time consultation
- Peer review of decisions
- Collaborative problem-solving
- Knowledge sharing

## How It Works
- Post questions/decisions to the board
- Peers can respond with their perspective
- All discussions are logged to 2535 for future reference
- Board posts are tagged with type: INFO, ASK, RESULT, HOLD, VETO

## Status
Ready for use. Board is active.
